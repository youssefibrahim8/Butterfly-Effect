How it works

  1. Step 1 Our model reads your concern

    A classifier trained on 154 example concerns picks the closest of five paths, or none.

  2. Step 2 The agent asks if it's unsure

    When two paths are close, it asks you one question instead of guessing.

  3. Step 3 You get a cited brief

    Real sources, what's uncertain, and one low-cost next step. Then share it with neighbours.

Results are leads to look into, not proven causes or guaranteed fixes.
